FROM THE FIRST IDEA TO THE FINAL DETAIL

A considered journey.
Made clear.

Good design takes more than a good idea. Here is how a project can move forward, with the right conversations along the way.

This is an illustrative process. The actual stages, deliverables, fees and programme depend on the studio, property and agreed scope.

01Initial enquiry

Tell us what you have in mind

Share your location, property details and the spaces you want to change.

02Discovery consultation

A conversation about you

Discuss everyday routines, tastes, priorities and how you want the space to feel.

03Site visit & measurements

Understand the site

Arrange access, take measurements and note the existing services and site conditions.

04Requirements & budget

Agree on the brief

Set priorities and an indicative budget before design work begins. Confirm consultation and design fees.

05Concept development

Find a design direction

Explore moodboards, references and a material language that fits the brief.

06Space planning

Make the layout work

Resolve circulation, furniture positions, storage and practical room relationships.

07Material selection

Make considered selections

Compare finishes, hardware and lighting with samples and maintenance in mind.

08Design presentation

See the design together

Review the proposed drawings and visualisations. Agree on revisions and approval milestones.

09Quotation & approval

Know what is included

Approve an itemised quotation, exclusions, payment stages and a project-specific programme.

10Production & execution

Bring the plan to life

Coordinate procurement, production, trades and site progress against the approved scope.

11Quality inspection

Check the details

Review finishes, alignment and function; record items that need attention before handover.

12Project handover

Make yourself at home

Walk through the space, close the agreed snag list and receive care and supplier documentation.
