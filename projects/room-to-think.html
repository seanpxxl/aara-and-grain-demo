THE IDEA BEHIND THE SPACE

Every room begins
with a reason.

The imagined brief

An imagined small-team office with flexible workstations, a meeting area and a welcoming place for visitors.

The design challenge

Provide useful separation between collaborative and focused work without making a modest office feel enclosed.

A proposed direction

Plan the work settings around noise, movement and daylight. Use a restrained palette and furniture that can respond to changing team needs.
